Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

Overview

Hide My Text is designed as a local-first privacy app. It protects supported content on the device before the user sends the resulting protected output through a separate platform of their choice.

Information Hide My Text requires

Hide My Text does not require a Hide My Text account, email address, phone number, or server login. We do not operate a Hide My Text messaging server that stores users' messages, working encryption keys, contacts, vaults, recovery phrases, or protected content.

Information stored locally

The app may store vault data, cryptographic keys, contact records, trust status, group or broadcast records, settings, and security preferences on the user's device. A user may also create and manually store an encrypted backup outside the app.

Protected content and external platforms

Content is protected locally by Hide My Text. When a user sends the protected output through another app or service, that external provider processes the delivery under its own terms and privacy policy. Hide My Text provides the local protection layer before that handoff.

Backups and recovery

Recovery remains user-controlled. The recovery phrase and manually stored encrypted backup are the user's recovery sources and should be kept securely in separate locations.

Apple services

Hide My Text is distributed through Apple's App Store services. Apple may process download, purchase, diagnostic, or account information under Apple's own policies. App Store distribution remains separate from the user's local vault contents, private keys, recovery phrase, protected content, and manually stored backup.

Children

Hide My Text is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact wearehidemytext@gmail.com.
