Use of Hide My Text

Hide My Text is provided to help users protect supported content locally before sending it through a separate platform. Users are responsible for how they use the app and for complying with applicable laws and platform rules.

Local data and recovery

Users control their vault, recovery phrase, contacts, keys, protected output, and exported backups. Recovery is based on the recovery phrase and encrypted backup retained by the user.

External delivery

Users choose the external service that delivers protected output. The use and availability of that delivery service are governed by its provider.

If paid plans or trials are offered, the exact availability, duration, price, renewal terms, and eligibility are shown by Apple at purchase time. Purchases and subscription management are processed through the App Store and can be managed in the user's Apple account settings.

Service availability

The app is provided as is. Availability and suitability may vary by device, operating-system conditions, and use case. Users should keep secure copies of required recovery material and evaluate whether the app fits their intended use.
