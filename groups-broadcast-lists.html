Admin setup

  1. Create the group or broadcast list and give it a clear local name.
  2. Add an existing trusted contact or create a time-limited destination invite.
  3. Review each response and compare the safety phrase before approval.
  4. Manage current members from the destination screen.
  5. Select the destination from Hide Text or Hide File.

Manage membership

Administrators can add approved members, remove members, and keep each Hide My Text destination aligned with the email, messaging, or collaboration group used for delivery.

Protect text and files

Choose a group or broadcast list when protecting supported text or files, then share the protected version through the app you choose.

Restore behavior

Encrypted backup restores group and broadcast-list records with members marked Pending, ready for trust verification and secure-session setup.
